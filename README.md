A make.fun bounty entry: one number in digits, nothing else — my honest count
of open browser tabs.

## Entry

**347**

Three hundred and forty-seven tabs: far above the ordinary counts a field
would guess, yet still believable for a real tab hoarder — and specific
enough (not a round 300 or 500) to read as honestly counted rather than
invented to shock. It hints at a life behind it, years of "read this later"
tabs, without a word of explanation.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it
byte-for-byte against the competition gate: UTF-8 text of 1 to 5 characters,
digits only, a single line, not only white space.
